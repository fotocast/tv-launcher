TV Launcher

Start Joyn shows and movies, YouTube channels and videos, and your own links with one click on a Mac that is connected to your TV – controlled from your laptop or iPhone.

On the next screen, choose what to install on this Mac:

  • App (remote control) – for the Mac you sit at.
  • Server – only for the Mac connected to the TV. It starts automatically at login and also serves the iPhone web app.

You can install both on the same Mac. Requires macOS 13 Ventura or later, Intel or Apple silicon. The app's interface is currently in German.
